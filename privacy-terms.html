Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ElevateLI has no servers

ElevateLI operates entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your LinkedIn data to our servers because we don't have any servers. Your profile data stays on your device unless you turn on AI analysis. Then the profile sections being analyzed are sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose, never to us.

What we collect

What stays on your device

  • LinkedIn profile data: All profile information analyzed by the extension remains locally in your browser, unless you turn on AI analysis (see AI analysis below)
  • Analysis results: Completeness scores and AI feedback are stored only in your browser's local storage
  • API keys: Your AI provider API keys (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic) are encrypted and stored locally in your browser
  • Settings: Your preferences and configuration are saved locally

What we never collect

  • Your LinkedIn profile information
  • Names, job titles, or any personal details
  • Your connections or network information
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Your API keys or AI responses

How your data is processed

Local analysis

The completeness scoring happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No external servers are involved in calculating your profile score.

AI analysis (optional)

If you choose to turn on AI analysis:

  • Your profile data (the sections being analyzed, including the text and recommender names of recommendations you received) is sent directly from your browser to your chosen AI provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or an OpenAI-compatible server whose address you enter, such as a local Ollama or LM Studio)
  • We never see or intercept this communication
  • The API request uses your personal API key
  • You're bound by your AI provider's privacy policy and terms

Data storage

Browser local storage

We use Chrome's local storage API to save:

  • Your latest analysis results, so they show again without another run
  • Your encrypted API keys
  • Extension settings and preferences
  • Your setup confirmation: the date and time you confirmed, and the version of the Terms you agreed to

This data is:

  • Stored only on your device
  • Never transmitted to external servers
  • Cleared when you uninstall the extension
  • Can be deleted at any time with Reset ElevateLI in ElevateLI settings

Third-party services

LinkedIn

The extension reads data from LinkedIn pages you visit. We don't modify or interfere with LinkedIn's own data collection practices. ElevateLI is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.

AI providers (optional)

If you turn on AI analysis, you're directly communicating with:

  • OpenAI: OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic: Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini: Google Privacy Policy / Gemini API Terms. With a free-tier Gemini key, Google may use what you send to improve its products.

Security measures

  • Encryption: API keys are encrypted using Web Crypto API before storage
  • No external communication: The extension doesn't communicate with any servers we control
  • Open source: anyone can read the code on GitHub
  • Minimal permissions: We only request necessary Chrome permissions

Your rights and controls

You have complete control over your data:

  • Reset ElevateLI (Settings): deletes every saved result, your API key and your settings
  • Remove API key (Settings): deletes your stored key and turns AI analysis off
  • Uninstall: Removes all extension data from your browser
  • Turn off AI analysis: ElevateLI then sends nothing outside LinkedIn

Children's privacy

ElevateLI is intended for professional use by individuals 16 years or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this privacy policy as needed and list significant changes on the ElevateLI welcome page, which opens after major updates.

Questions about privacy?

Open a GitHub issue (free account needed). Issues are public, so don't include personal details.
