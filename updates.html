Quick start

1
Open my LinkedIn profile
2
Click Analyze in the ElevateLI bar on your profile, or pin ElevateLI from the puzzle-piece (Extensions) menu and choose Get started
3
Your page scrolls for a few seconds while ElevateLI reads each section, then your completeness score appears.
4
Fill in missing sections first (each tip shows the % it adds)

What you get

✓ Completeness score in one click
✓ Tips for each section
✓ No account. ElevateLI collects nothing.

AI ratings and writing tips

Optional
Add your own Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic API key, or a free local model (Ollama or LM Studio), in ElevateLI settings to get:
  • An AI rating (1–5 stars) for each section
  • Writing tips for your target role and level
How AI analysis works:
✓ Requests go straight from your browser to your provider
✓ Your API key is encrypted and stays in this browser
✓ Turn AI analysis off anytime

Your provider bills you directly for AI use. Local models are free.
